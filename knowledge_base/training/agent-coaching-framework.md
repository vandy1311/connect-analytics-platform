# Agent Coaching Framework

## Purpose
This framework guides QA analysts and team leads in providing effective coaching based on Contact Lens sentiment analysis and quality scorecards.

## Coaching Triggers
An agent qualifies for coaching when:
- Negative sentiment rate exceeds 15% over a 7-day period
- 3 or more negative sentiment calls in a single week
- Customer escalation rate exceeds 10%
- Quality scorecard drops below 75%

## Coaching Categories

### De-escalation Coaching
**When:** Agent has pattern of customer escalations or negative sentiment from frustrated customers
**Focus areas:**
- Active listening — let the customer finish before responding
- Empathy statements: "I understand how frustrating this must be"
- Avoid interrupting — this is the #1 driver of negative sentiment
- Offer concrete next steps, not vague promises
**Duration:** 30-minute 1:1 session + 1 week of monitored calls

### Hold Management Training
**When:** Agent has complaints about long hold times or lack of status updates
**Focus areas:**
- Never place on hold without explaining why and estimated duration
- Check back every 60 seconds during holds
- Offer callback option for holds exceeding 2 minutes
- Thank the customer for their patience when returning
**Duration:** 15-minute refresher module

### Empathy and Active Listening
**When:** Agent has consistently neutral or negative sentiment scores
**Focus areas:**
- Mirror the customer's language and tone
- Acknowledge the problem before jumping to solutions
- Use the customer's name at least twice during the call
- Summarize the issue back to confirm understanding
**Duration:** 45-minute workshop + peer shadowing

### Script Compliance
**When:** Agent deviates from required scripts (payment, disclosure, greeting)
**Focus areas:**
- Review required script elements
- Practice natural delivery of scripted content
- Understand WHY each element is required (compliance, legal)
**Duration:** 20-minute review session

## Coaching Effectiveness Tracking
- Re-evaluate sentiment scores 2 weeks after coaching
- Target: 10+ percentage point improvement in negative sentiment rate
- If no improvement after 2 coaching sessions, escalate to team lead

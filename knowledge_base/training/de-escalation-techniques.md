# De-escalation Training Module

## The HEARD Framework
Use this 5-step framework when a customer is upset or escalating:

### H — Hear them out
- Let the customer speak without interruption
- Take notes on key points
- Do NOT say "I understand" until they've finished

### E — Empathize
- "I can see why that would be frustrating"
- "That's not the experience we want you to have"
- Match their energy level — if they're upset, show urgency

### A — Apologize (when appropriate)
- "I'm sorry this happened" — even if it's not your fault
- Apologize for the experience, not necessarily the policy
- Be genuine — customers detect scripted apologies

### R — Resolve
- Offer a concrete solution or next step
- Give a specific timeline: "I'll have this resolved within 2 hours"
- If you can't resolve immediately, explain exactly what happens next

### D — Document
- Log the interaction details in the CRM
- Note the resolution or escalation path
- Flag for follow-up if needed

## Common Mistakes
1. **Interrupting** — the #1 cause of escalation. Let them finish.
2. **Saying "calm down"** — never works, always makes it worse
3. **Blaming other departments** — the customer doesn't care whose fault it is
4. **Reading from a script robotically** — be natural, be human
5. **Rushing to a solution** — sometimes they just need to be heard first

## When to Escalate to Supervisor
- Customer explicitly requests a supervisor
- Threat of legal action or regulatory complaint
- You've attempted resolution twice and customer remains unsatisfied
- The issue requires authority beyond your level (refunds > $500, account closures)
